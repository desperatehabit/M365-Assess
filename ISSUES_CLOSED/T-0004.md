# T-0004 — Shared helper and connector state moves into RunContext

## Report

`SecurityConfigHelper.ps1:32` creates `$global:AdoptionSignals` and `:41` sets
`$script:ActiveSecurityConfig`; `Connect-Service.ps1` and `Test-GraphPermissions.ps1` read
caller-scope state. `01-architecture.md` §4 and EPIC-001 SPEC.md §4.1 name these as the
process-global surface that must collapse into `$ctx`.

Deliver:

- The collector helper locates its settings collection and adoption signals via the active
  RunContext rather than `$global:`/`$script:` globals, while keeping the frozen collector
  contract intact: `Initialize-SecurityConfig`, `Add-Setting`, `Export-SecurityConfigReport`
  and the nine-status taxonomy do not change (05-programming.md §2.1).
- `Connect-RequiredService`'s service-state sets and the Graph-permission/licence-reconciliation
  flags resolve from `$ctx.Services`.
- Because collectors dot-source this helper per run, sequential collectors stay isolated
  without cross-run bleed.

## Acceptance

- [ ] No `$global:AdoptionSignals` / `$script:ActiveSecurityConfig` remains as process state.
- [ ] Collector output shape and all nine statuses are unchanged; a test asserts the contract.
- [ ] A test proves two sequential contexts do not share settings or signals.
- [ ] Existing collector suites pass.
- [ ] No file outside `scope` is modified.
