# T-0504 — Create team with local template expansion

## Report

EPIC-026 SPEC.md §2 US-2, §3.1, and §4.1 define team creation: an `Add team` wizard takes name,
owners, members, template, and visibility, create routes through **EPIC-006**, and creation from
a template expands owners/members. §6 exposes `POST /v1/tenants/{id}/teams`. §11 item 3 is
resolved (adopted): team templates are **local** (`TeamTemplate`, T-0501) in v1; community
template import/export is handed to EPIC-039.

Deliver a `New-Team.ps1` handler (entrypoint `new-team.ps1`) that accepts the wizard fields,
resolves a stored `TeamTemplate` by id and expands its owners/members/settings, and applies
through the EPIC-006 gated executor (T-0108) with audit, returning a `ResultEnvelope`. The route
validates `teams.write` + `Remediation.Apply` and tenant scope, and records a `TeamOperation`.
Extend `portal.v1.yaml`.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Create accepts name/owners/members/template/visibility and routes through EPIC-006.
- [ ] A supplied `TeamTemplate` expands its owners/members/settings into the new team.
- [ ] The operation is audited as a `TeamOperation`; the route refuses callers without `teams.write`.
- [ ] A test covers create with and without a template and fails without it.
- [ ] No file outside `scope` is modified.
