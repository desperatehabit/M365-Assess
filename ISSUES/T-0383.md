# T-0383 — Mailbox settings: quotas, archive, and holds

## Report

EPIC-020 SPEC.md §2 (US-3), §3.2, §4.1, and §6 require `PATCH /v1/tenants/{id}/mailboxes/{mailboxId}`
to apply quota, archive (including auto-expanding), litigation and retention holds, locale,
recipient limits, calendar processing, and hide-from-GAL settings. §4.1 makes every operation
*form → plan preview → apply* through the EPIC-006 gated executor; §9 risk "quota/archive changes
affecting users" is mitigated by plan preview and confirmation.

Add the settings handler to `mailboxes.ts` and the `Set-MailboxSettings.ps1` worker job plus
`set-mailbox-settings.ps1` entrypoint. Each apply records a `MailboxOperation` (T-0382) and an
`AuditEvent` with before/after; enabling or expanding archive and changing a hold require explicit
confirmation. Extend `portal.v1.yaml`. Tenant and credential records are EPIC-002 (prose
dependency).

## Acceptance

- [ ] Quota, archive (incl. auto-expanding), litigation hold, and retention hold settings apply with before/after captured.
- [ ] Locale, recipient limits, calendar processing, and hide-from-GAL apply through the same gated path.
- [ ] Every apply writes a `MailboxOperation` and an `AuditEvent`.
- [ ] A test covers the change and fails without it.
- [ ] No file outside `scope` is modified.
