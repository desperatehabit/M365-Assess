# T-0123 — Scheduler tick, system-timer registry, and single-flight enqueue

## Report

EPIC-007 SPEC.md §4.2: a scheduler tick evaluates due `Schedule` rows and enqueues a `Job`
processed by the same worker pool as EPIC-003 (T-0010). §4.1 lists the code-deployed system
timers (standards every 12 h, drift every 12 h +15 min, webhooks every 15 min, cleanup, token
refresh). §9 risk "scheduler drift/overlap" is mitigated by **single-flight per schedule** (skip
or queue). §11.4 resolves job persistence to **in-memory first** for single-node, durable later
(`03-database.md` §2).

Deliver:

- `system-timers.ts`: the registry of built-in timers (name, cron, type) as code-deployed,
  read-only definitions.
- `tick.ts`: evaluates due user schedules and system timers, enqueues through the T-0010 queue
  with `trigger: schedule` and the schedule id, updates `lastRunAt`/`nextRunAt`, and enforces
  single-flight (a schedule already running is skipped, not double-enqueued).
- Tests using a fake clock for due evaluation, enqueue-once, and overlap suppression.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A due schedule enqueues exactly one job with `trigger: schedule`.
- [ ] A schedule already running is not enqueued again on the next tick.
- [ ] `lastRunAt`/`nextRunAt` update after the job's outcome is recorded.
- [ ] A test covers the change and fails without it.
- [ ] No file outside `scope` is modified.
