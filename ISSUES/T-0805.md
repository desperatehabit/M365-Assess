# T-0805 — CSP licensing adapter (deferred)

## Report

**PARKED / deferred.** EPIC-041 SPEC.md §1, §2 (US-2), §3.1. The CSP licensing connector
(Sherweb) is **deferred** until demand names the CSP and the licence-source-of-truth semantics
are agreed; this ticket is optional and stays behind the parked epic.

Deliver the vendor-neutral `csp-adapter.ts` through the T-0801 registry: licensing read/mapping
config, a `test` handshake, and a `sync` entry point that is a no-op until a CSP adapter is
registered. Licence data is read-only against the vendor and merged with the portal's existing
licence resolution (EPIC-002's credential flow); no vendor HTTP is added here.
