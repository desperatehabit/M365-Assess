# T-0508 — Business voice phone numbers and policy assignment (license-gated)

## Report

EPIC-026 SPEC.md §2 US-4, §3.3, §4.3, and §8 define **Teams Business Voice**: a phone-number
inventory, assignment to users/resource accounts, and voice-policy assignment. Voice is heavily
license-gated, so the page shows a clear message when voice is not licensed. §6 exposes
`GET`/`POST`/`DELETE` under `/v1/tenants/{id}/teams/voice/numbers`; §8 routes assign/release
through **EPIC-006** with confirmation on release and audit. §11 item 1 is resolved (adopted):
voice is in v1 but **license-gated**, with Teams core shipped first.

Deliver an `Invoke-TeamsVoice.ps1` handler (entrypoint `invoke-teams-voice.ps1`) for list,
assign, release, and policy assignment, applying writes through the EPIC-006 gated executor
(T-0108) and recording a `TeamOperation` (T-0501). The route validates `teams.voice` +
`Remediation.Apply`, requires confirmation on release, and audits every change; the page renders
`VoiceNumbers` and the not-licensed state. Extend `portal.v1.yaml`.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Number inventory, assign, release, and policy assignment render and operate.
- [ ] Release requires confirmation; every change is audited as a `TeamOperation`.
- [ ] When voice is unlicensed the page shows a clear requirement message and the route refuses writes.
- [ ] A test covers the license gate and release confirmation and fails without it.
- [ ] No file outside `scope` is modified.
