# T-0565 — Email and webhook delivery adapters with retry and meta-alert

## Report

EPIC-029 SPEC.md §3.4 and §4.3 define delivery: per-channel adapters — email (SMTP/Graph), webhook
(HTTP POST), PSA (create ticket), Slack (blocks) — with failures retried and logged, and a channel
failure raising a **meta-alert**. §11.2 is resolved to make **email + webhook the first delivery
channels**; §11.3 defers PSA to EPIC-041; Slack remains future. §9 requires channel credentials to
be stored **by reference** and redacted in logs.

Deliver the `AlertDelivery` adapter contract and the email + webhook implementations: a
`deliver(event, channelConfig)` result, bounded retry with backoff, redaction of secrets/targets in
logs, and emission of the delivery-failure meta-alert through the same rule pipeline. Webhook URLs
and email recipients are read from `NotificationConfig` (T-0561); credentials resolve by reference
via the EPIC-002 credential store (prose dependency).

## Acceptance

- [ ] Email and webhook adapters deliver a payload and report success/failure per attempt.
- [ ] A failed delivery retries with bounded backoff and raises the meta-alert once.
- [ ] Secrets and targets never appear unredacted in logs or error output.
- [ ] PSA and Slack are not implemented here (PSA deferred to EPIC-041).
- [ ] Tests cover success, retry, and meta-alert paths and fail without the code.
- [ ] No file outside `scope` is modified.
