# T-0386 — Vacation mode schedules with auto-revert

## Report

EPIC-020 SPEC.md §2 (US-6), §3.5, §4.3, §5, and §6 require scheduling OoO and forwarding for a
window, listing active/upcoming schedules, and an automatic revert at the window end with a
manual **End now**. Resolved §11.2 adopts **reuse of the EPIC-007 scheduler** (cron contracts
T-0121, schedule storage T-0122, tick/single-flight T-0123) for enable and revert rather than a
new engine; §9 risk "vacation auto-revert failure" is mitigated by a scheduled job plus alert on
failure.

Deliver migration `0032_vacation_schedules.sql` for the §5 `VacationSchedule` entity
(`id`, `tenantId`, `mailboxId`, `startsAt`, `endsAt`, `oooMessage`, `forwardTo`, `state`) and
`vacation-repository.ts`. Add `vacation-schedules.ts` for the §6 `GET`/`POST`/`DELETE` routes and
the `Invoke-VacationSchedule.ps1` worker job plus `invoke-vacation-schedule.ps1` entrypoint that
enables at start and reverts at end through the EPIC-006 gate (T-0107), writing a
`MailboxOperation` (T-0382) and an `AuditEvent` for both the apply and the revert. Register the
revert as a scheduler job via the EPIC-007 tick; extend `portal.v1.yaml`. Tenant and credential
records are EPIC-002 (prose dependency).

## Acceptance

- [ ] A vacation schedule persists with start/end, OoO message, and forwarding target, and lists as active/upcoming.
- [ ] The EPIC-007 scheduler enables the schedule at start and reverts it at end; the revert is audited.
- [ ] Manual **End now** reverts immediately and is audited.
- [ ] A failed revert is recorded and raises an alert rather than silently ending.
- [ ] A test covers the change and fails without it.
- [ ] No file outside `scope` is modified.
