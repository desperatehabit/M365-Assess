## Report

actions (`View item`, `Remove link`, `Open in SharePoint`), and the gated bulk action (`Remove
selected links`). Nav follows *Teams & SharePoint → Sharing Report* (§3). §11 item 2 is resolved

plus a first-class `RiskyLinksView` for anonymous/organization links. `Remove link` / `Remove
selected links` hand off to the bulk-removal ticket (T-0527/T-0528) without performing removal
here. A Vitest/Testing-Library test covers loading, loaded, empty, the risky view, and filters.
