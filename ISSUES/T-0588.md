# T-0588 — Purview Compliance UI: DLP, Retention, and Safe Links

## Report

EPIC-030 SPEC.md §3.1/§3.2/§3.5 define the DLP, Retention, and Safe Links policy tables with their
row actions and the **plan preview** in the policy editors; §4.1 requires preview before apply. Nav
is *Security & Compliance → Purview Compliance* (DLP, Retention) and *Safe Links* per
[`cipp-ui-inventory.md`](../../docs/portal-specs/99-reference/cipp-ui-inventory.md) §3. Theme per
[`02-ui-design.md`](../../docs/portal-specs/00-guides/02-ui-design.md). §8 requires the disable flow
to show the compliance warning and confirmation.

Deliver the three v1-area pages wired to the T-0582/T-0584/T-0585 read routes and the
T-0582/T-0583/T-0584/T-0585 change routes: tables with the specified columns, row actions, and a
plan-preview drawer that shows before/after and the compliance-impacting warning before apply.

## Acceptance

- [ ] DLP, Retention, and Safe Links pages render the §3.1/§3.2/§3.5 columns and row actions.
- [ ] The editor shows a plan preview (before/after) before apply.
- [ ] Disabling DLP/retention shows the compliance warning and requires confirmation.
- [ ] A test covers rendering and the preview/confirm flow and fails without it.
- [ ] No file outside `scope` is modified.
