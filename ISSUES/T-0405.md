# T-0405 — Connector template storage and CRUD

## Report

EPIC-021 SPEC.md §2 (US-4), §3.3, §5, and §6 require connector templates with `View`, `Edit`,
`Clone`, `Deploy`, `Export`, and `Delete`. §5 defines `ConnectorTemplate` (`id`, `name`,
`connectorJson`, `variables`, `source`). Resolved §11.3 adopts **local templates first**, deferring
community sharing to EPIC-039.

Deliver migration `0035_connector_templates.sql` and extend the shared
`transport-template-repository.ts` (T-0403) with the connector-template methods, plus
`connector-templates.ts` for the §6 `GET`/`POST`/`PATCH`/`DELETE` routes. Templates persist;
`connectorJson` may carry only **references** to connector secrets, never secret material
(T-0404). Extend `portal.v1.yaml`. Deploy itself is T-0406.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Migration 0035 applies after 0034, is re-runnable, and advances `SchemaVersion`.
- [ ] Connector templates create, read, update, soft-delete, and clone.
- [ ] A persisted `connectorJson` carries secret references only, never secret values.
- [ ] A test covers the change and fails without it.
- [ ] No file outside `scope` is modified.
